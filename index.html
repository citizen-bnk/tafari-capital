Tafari Minerals Exchange

From first document to final shipment

The mineral market is full of recycled rights, borrowed mandates and tonnages nobody has drilled. The Exchange is built to filter those out before a buyer spends time or money.

  1. ListThe owner registers, gives a mandate and uploads documents.
  2. VerifyEach document is checked with the issuing regulator or an independent expert.
  3. ScoreThe asset gets a risk rating. Open red flags block release.
  4. Deal roomVetted buyers see verified assets under NCNDA and make offers.
  5. TransactThe asset moves into an SPV for a partial stake or full buyout.
  6. OperateEquipment, contract miners and processing partners are engaged.
  7. ExportTransport, port, customs and shipping through to the buyer.

Checked in South Africa

  • Company and directors at CIPC
  • Mining or prospecting right with the DMPR and on SAMRAD
  • Environmental authorisation and water use licence
  • Tax compliance status with SARS
  • B-BBEE and Mining Charter status
  • SAMREC Competent Person's Report and lab assays

Checked in Lesotho

  • Company and shareholders with the Registrar of Companies
  • Mining lease or licence with the Department of Mines
  • Mining Board and Ministerial approvals
  • Environmental clearance
  • Tax clearance with the Lesotho Revenue Authority
  • Independent resource report and assays
Enter the Exchange Tokenized participation will be offered only through appropriately licensed partners, and only after the required regulatory and Ministerial approvals are in place.

How we work

Rules we keep on every deal

These are the conditions under which Tafari puts its name to a transaction.

  1. No mandate, no listingWe act only for owners who can show the right and a signed authority to deal.
  2. Claims are checked at sourceA right described as granted needs a grant, not an application receipt.
  3. No data pack before an NCNDATechnical data is shared only under signed confidentiality and with the owner's consent.
  4. Buyers are vetted tooKYC and proof of funds come before site visits and detailed due diligence.
  5. Regulators approve ownership changesTransfers and changes of control wait for the required Ministerial consent.
  6. Fees are agreed in writingOur fee, who pays it and when is set out before introductions are made.
